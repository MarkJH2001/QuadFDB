Research figures, media and aggregate results
The authors' platform, flight response and dataset coverage figures are
rendered as web images. The demonstration video preserves the existing
measured-pose replay and real-flight camera comparison.
Source identities and transformations are documented in
assets/source-provenance.json.

The site contains aggregate evaluation results and presentation media.
Dataset and code access is provided through the project repository:
